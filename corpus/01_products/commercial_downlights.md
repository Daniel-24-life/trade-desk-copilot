# LuminaTech Architectural Deep Anti-Glare Downlight Series

## Product Overview & Design Highlights
The **LT-DL01 Series** is engineered for high-end hospitality, boutique retail, commercial offices, and luxury residential projects. Designed with a deep-recessed optical chamber and interchangeable secondary reflector rings (matte black, specular chrome, rose gold, and pure white), it achieves excellent visual comfort with a unified glare rating **UGR < 19**.

- **Optical Engine**: Citizen / Bridgelux COB LED array, CRI Ra > 92 (R9 > 50), MacAdam 3-step ellipse color consistency.
- **Thermal Management**: Cold-forged ADC12 die-cast aluminum heatsink ensuring $T_j < 75^\circ\text{C}$ and $L_{80}B_{10} > 50,000$ hours.
- **Beam Angles**: 15°, 24°, 38°, 60° interchangeable TIR lenses or faceted specular reflectors.
