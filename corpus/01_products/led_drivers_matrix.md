# LuminaTech ProDrive Constant Current LED Driver Series

## Technical Features & Engineering Value
The **LT-DRV ProDrive Series** is built for mission-critical architectural lighting projects, commercial office fixtures, and studio lighting where zero optical flicker and multi-protocol control are mandated.

- **Ripple-Free Technology**: Ripple current < 1%, compliance with IEEE 1789 standard (no stroboscopic effect, perfect for HD video recording and eye-care environments).
- **Selectable Output Current**: Built-in 4-position DIP switch allows on-site selection across 300mA, 500mA, 700mA, 900mA, and 1050mA.
- **Power Factor & Efficiency**: $\text{PF} > 0.95$ at 230VAC full load; conversion efficiency up to 89%.
- **Protections**: Short circuit, over current, over voltage, and thermal foldback protection with auto-recovery.
