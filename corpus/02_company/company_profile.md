# LuminaTech Global Lighting Solutions Ltd.

## Enterprise Overview
LuminaTech Global Lighting Solutions Ltd. is a specialized OEM/ODM manufacturer of commercial architectural lighting, smart residential fixtures, and precision LED drivers, headquartered in Shenzhen with a 12,000 m² modern manufacturing facility. With 12 years of export experience across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia, LuminaTech provides end-to-end engineering support from optical simulation (DIALux/IES) to customized mold tooling.

## Verification & Compliance Backing
- **Factory Verification**: Verified Manufacturer & Gold Supplier, audited by SGS / TÜV Rheinland.
- **Quality Management**: ISO 9001:2015 Certified Quality System.
- **Safety & EMC Certifications**: CE (EMC/LVD), RoHS 2.0, FCC Part 15B, UL Recognized Driver Components.
- **Reliability Testing**: 100% 4-hour full-load burn-in aging test for all finished fixtures before packing; IP65 waterproof chamber testing for outdoor/bathroom series.
- **Optical Testing**: In-house integrating sphere and distributed photometer with full IES / LDT report generation.
